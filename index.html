GHOSTPROOF

a small zero-knowledge memory game, built while waiting on a whitelist

The verifier shows you a hidden pattern for a moment, then hides it again. A moment later it asks about only some of the cells. Prove you remember exactly which of those were real — reveal nothing about the rest.

Five rounds. Get four right and your identity is verified.
